Profile

I combine 11 years of field experience as a CT Customer Service Engineer at Siemens Healthineers Korea with doctoral training in biomedical engineering. My research focuses on quantitative chest CT, radiomics, and the relationship between CT-derived features and pulmonary function.

Siemens Healthineers Korea에서 11년간 CT 시스템의 설치·유지보수·품질관리를 수행한 경험과 의공학 박사과정을 바탕으로, 흉부 CT 정량영상, radiomics 및 CT 기반 폐기능 분석을 연구하고 있습니다.
